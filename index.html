02 / Practice

A research-to-design loop.

My work starts with the learning problem and keeps evidence, context and ethics visible throughout.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Surface learner experience, context, constraints and the real problem behind the request.

  2. 02

    Frame

    Connect observations to research, define a question and make assumptions explicit.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    Build the smallest experience that can make an idea concrete and discussable.

  4. 04

    Learn

    Gather feedback, examine limitations and refine the system without overstating results.

04 / About

Educator’s instinct.
Researcher’s discipline.
Designer’s curiosity.

I am a learning architect and educational-technology professional with a foundation in language teaching and qualitative research.

Across teaching, research and prototyping, I keep returning to educational inequality created by rigid curricula, weak transitions between learning environments and systems that fail to respond to learner variability. I am most useful where evidence needs to become a clear learning experience, product direction or programme story.
